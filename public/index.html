What is Bald?

The story

BALD is a meme-inspired cryptocurrency that made waves as the first memecoin deployed on Base. Its name is a playful tribute to Brian Armstrong, the bald CEO of Coinbase, further adding to its meme appeal.

As the pioneer memecoin on Base, BALD quickly gained attention due to its humorous reference to Armstrong and its association with the broader Coinbase ecosystem. This unique provenance has cemented its status as a cultural phenomenon.

The legendary Jesse Pollak (VP, Engineering at Coinbase) bought $BALD in the open market. He chose this BALD and not any other, confirming what everyone already believes: this is the OG BALD. Its connection to Coinbase’s ecosystem and its undeniable influence make BALD more than just a meme.

Don’t fall for rugs or copycats

This is the OG bald

First BALD deployed on Base was rugged by the dev, as shown in the attached tweet. It’s still trading and can be found on Dexscreener, but the chart is incomplete since the rug happened on LeetSwap, not Uniswap, which can also cause some confusion. Additionally, its liquidity cannot be trusted, as the dev could withdraw it at any moment.

From the ashes of that rug, this BALD emerged, created by the community to ensure such a betrayal could never happen again. It has survived and thrived ever since, growing steadily.

Other copycats have appeared, but this BALD is the one that immediately rose after the original rug and has stood the test of time, a symbol of resilience and collective effort.
